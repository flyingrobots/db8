## Context

What situation forced a choice. Include the constraints that were real at the
time — deadlines, existing data, a defect already in production — because those
are what a later reader cannot reconstruct.

State the problem, not the solution. If this section could be written without
knowing which option was picked, it is doing its job.

## Decision

What was chosen, in the active voice: _"Persistence adapters are selected by
configuration."_ One decision per ADR.

## Consequences

What follows, **including the bad parts**. An ADR listing only benefits is
marketing and will not be trusted the next time.

- What becomes easier.
- What becomes harder.
- What is now load-bearing that was not before.
- What has to be true for this to keep working.

## Alternatives considered

Each one that was genuinely on the table, and the specific reason it lost. "We
did not consider alternatives" is an acceptable and useful answer.
